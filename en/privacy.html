Privacy policy

How QUAI 46 handles your personal data under Swiss revFADP and the GDPR.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. In short

We collect as little data as possible. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. We only process your data if you join the waiting list or write to us, and we never sell it.

2. Data controller

QUAI 46 — [Company name / owner]
[Street and number]
[Postcode] Geneva, Switzerland
Email: contact@quai46.ch

This policy applies under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP, in force since 1 September 2023) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

3. What we process and why

Visiting the website

When you visit the website, our host GitHub records technical data (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser) in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. We have no access to these logs. Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Waiting list

If you sign up, we process your email address, chosen language, sign-up date and consent, solely to inform you about our drops and the opening of the shop. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time.

Contact form and email

If you write to us, we process your name, email address and the content of your message in order to reply. Legal basis: your request and our legitimate interest in answering it (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

4. Service providers and transfers abroad

We use the following providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Formspree, Inc. (United States) — receiving form submissions (waiting list and contact) and forwarding them by email. Formspree privacy policy.
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States), a Microsoft subsidiary — website hosting (GitHub Pages). GitHub privacy statement.
  • Our email provider — receiving and sending emails.

The United States does not, in general, provide a level of data protection equivalent to Switzerland or the EU. These transfers rely on the Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the standard contractual clauses recognised by the Swiss Federal Data Protection Commissioner (FDPIC) and the European Commission.

The links to Instagram and TikTok are plain links: no data is sent to them unless you click. Our typefaces are hosted on our own website; no data is sent to Google.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Waiting list: until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent, and no later than 24 months after your last interaction with us (sign-up, email exchange or placing an order).
  • Contact messages: 12 months after our exchange ends, unless a longer retention period is required by law (for example 10 years for accounting records relating to an order, Art. 958f Swiss Code of Obligations).
  • Copies held by Formspree: we export and then delete submissions from our Formspree account at least once a month.
  • GitHub technical logs: according to GitHub’s policy.

6. Your rights — and how to have your data deleted

You may at any time request access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing, object to processing, receive your data in a common format (portability) and withdraw your consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

To have your data deleted, simply email contact@quai46.ch with the subject “Delete my data”, from the address concerned. We will delete your data from our list, our mailbox and Formspree, and confirm this within 30 days at most. Every waiting-list email also contains an unsubscribe link.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, www.edoeb.admin.ch) or, if you live in the EU, the data protection authority in your country.

7. Security

The website is served over HTTPS only. Access to our accounts (GitHub, Formspree, email) is protected by two-factor authentication.

8. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the online shop opens. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.
