About

Geneva, Quai 46.

A clothing house born of a simple idea: fewer pieces, better made.

Origins

It all begins at the water’s edge. Between the Jet d’Eau and the Alps, Geneva cultivates a quiet elegance that never needs to raise its voice. QUAI 46 draws on that restraint to design clothes that are understated, precise and timeless.

Our approach

Inspired by Swiss design — clear grids, functional typography, an eye for detail — we refine proportions, materials and finishes until there is nothing left to take away.

Each drop is made in limited quantities with partner workshops in Europe, chosen for their craftsmanship and working conditions.

“Simplicity is not an absence. It is the result of a thousand decisions.”
